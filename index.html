Allow access to your music?

To play your MP3s, the browser needs your permission to read your files. Nothing is uploaded — everything stays on your phone / PC.

  1. Tap Load My Music below
  2. On your phone, pick your Music / Downloads folder or select MP3 files
  3. Tap Allow / Select — done! 🎧

💡 On Android Chrome: choose a folder to load hundreds of songs at once. On iPhone Safari: select multiple files. You can also drag & drop files anytime. Tip: on Chrome you can grant folder access once and the app will auto-scan it on every open.

📱 How to load music on your phone

  1. Android (Chrome): Tap Add Music → Choose Folder and pick Music or Download. Allow access. Grant once → auto-scan on every open.
  2. iPhone (Safari): Tap Add Music → Choose Files, go to Files → On My iPhone / iCloud and select MP3s. iOS limits folder picking — select files in batches.
  3. PC: Drag & drop MP3s anywhere, or use the file picker. Folder mode loads whole libraries.
  4. Cover art & tags are read automatically from ID3. Use Edit Tags on any song to fix # / artist / album / cover.

🎮 Dashboard tips

Albums are game cases — tap one for details, or scroll the carousel on Home. The ⋯ menu on any song gives queue, playlist, tag & share options. Radio lives in its own blade. The guide button (X) opens quick actions.

🔒 Privacy

Everything runs locally. Files, favorites & edits never leave your device. Radio streams are fetched directly from the station.
